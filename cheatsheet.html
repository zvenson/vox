FX3 sub-pages (chips, K5 again = next): DELAY = amount · time · feedback · kind (digital · analog · tape · ping-pong) · REVERB = amount · size · pre-delay (0–100 ms) · kind (hall · room · plate · spring) · MOD = amount (0 = off) · rate · depth · kind (chorus · flanger · phaser) – MOD sits on the voice before delay and reverb
LOOP4 loop tracks side by side (like a loop station). Here the pads are the looper, not presets: pads 1–4 (top row) and encoder push 1–4 (VIBE Box) = one switch per track: tap = empty → REC · REC → plays · plays → overdub · overdub → plays · stopped → plays in time · tap twice = STOP · hold 1.5 s = undo/redo (playing) or clear (stopped) · overdub starts on the press. Pads 5–8 (bottom row) = STOP at the loop's end (again = now) · hold = clear. The first loop sets the length (LENGTH: free or bars), the others start at its beginning (WAIT) and end on a multiple. Per track three faders: VOL · DLY (send 1 to the delay) · REV (send 2 to the reverb) – encoders 1–4 = tracks 1–4, the row is the chip on top (VOL · DLY · REV · LENGTH): K6 again = next row. LENGTH row: length · overdub · loops · REC FX – ON (default) = the looper records the voice with delay, reverb and MOD, as you hear it; OFF = before them. Choose a track without triggering it: tap SHIFT, then a pad (within 1.5 s) or hold SHIFT + pad on the VIBE · PLAY / REC / STOP / pedal / SHIFT+F6 act on the chosen track · SHIFT+STOP = all stop at the loop's end · SHIFT+REC = clear all · F5 SAVE WAV = the chosen track as a WAV

The four encoders under the display set the area's values.

Sub-pages: where an area has several, they are chips in the second header line – INPUT: LEVELS · SETUP (input, compressor kind, S-frequency) · EQ: BANDS · MID · VOX: OFF · TUNE · VOCODER · HARMONY · LOOP: VOL · DLY · REV · LENGTH. The same colour key again = next sub-page.

Mouse in the plugin and the browser: ◀ ▶ in the header = previous/next area, chips = sub-page, the F keys, preset cells, the looper's track switches, STOP fields, a waveform = choose that track, a fader = that row; drag on an encoder column = the encoder.

Hold SHIFT + turn DATA: a frame walks over every control of the page, then the F keys and the header · push the wheel = change it, push again = done · RETURN or 6 s untouched = gone, the wheel chooses the program again.
